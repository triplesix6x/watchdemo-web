## Oracle and scope limits

The interaction module asserts actual DOM behavior; screenshot existence alone is never acceptance. Generic route-mount rows deliberately claim only a main heading, not full page semantics. Fixed selectors are traced to existing `AuthCard`, `Feedback`, `AppShell`, `UiShowcase` and route sources, with browser verification limited to the mounted async/controls subset above. Other selectors remain browser-unverified. Failures must be investigated, not silently omitted or converted into new production behavior.

Three independent captures at the declared representative principal-layout noise probes characterize repeated unchanged noise. Single-capture cases do not independently measure noise. Diagnostic screenshots exist, but no reviewed golden files have been accepted. PNG/DOM hash equality is a conservative diagnostic, not a calibrated perceptual oracle; any repeat noise remains unresolved, and stable geometry/color/text/graphics differences require review. `accepting:false` is recorded in every manifest, including full representative runs. Parent inspects goldens and updates module dispositions separately.

Full mode checks completeness for this exported matrix only, including duplicate/missing/unexpected keys. Diagnostic/slice runs list the missing cases and cannot pass full acceptance. There is no claim that the exported matrix exhausts module-contracts. Cross-boundary transport/XHR/blob, head/SSR/prerender, focus preview, full mock integrity, every significant loading/empty/error state, legal evidence and 405-file registry acceptance remain deferred until their own executable gates exist.

Animation determinism remains a separate unresolved gate: normal animations remain enabled, no custom global WAAPI overrides are installed and no repeated-capture noise is accepted by this startup change. No known production bug, visual/a11y difference, graphics parameter or native-device policy is fixed by this package.
